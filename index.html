The Chef

Chef Bobba

"The sea is the most generous kitchen in the world. Every tide brings a new inspiration."

Born on the wind-scoured coast of Normandy, France, Jean-Baptiste "Bobba" Delacroix grew up gutting mackerel before he could read. At sixteen he crossed the English Channel to stage at a two-star kitchen in London; at twenty-four he won his first Michelin star in Paris. Today, after three decades along the coasts of France, Japan, and Peru, he holds three stars and a lifelong obsession with one ingredient: the ocean.

This collection is his living library — the dishes he returns to every season, refined over thousands of services and shared, for the first time, without reservation.
